## Compound phrases and priority

“简单但功能完整” usually means minimal UI retaining named functions, not dropping requirements. “详细但一句话” may be a real conflict. “便宜、快、最好” needs a priority or a labeled trade-off. Treat “尽量 / preferably” as softer than an explicit limit; preserve “必须 / must”.
Do not silently assume local conventions: ask or preserve ambiguity when relative dates, currency, locale, color meaning or date formats affect an important result. Use a reliable supplied timezone/date when available.
